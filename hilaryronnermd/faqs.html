At every annual well visit, most pediatricians check a child's vision. However, it is recommended that a complete exam by an ophthalmologist take place when the child starts pre-school.

Dr. Ronner will put drops in the child's eyes to view the retina and determine whether glasses are needed. If glasses are prescribed, annual visits are recommended; if not, an exam every 3–4 years is typically sufficient.

For a healthy patient with no medical problems, a full annual ophthalmologic exam should begin at age 35. If there is a family history of glaucoma or macular degeneration, exams should begin several years earlier. Early diagnosis of serious conditions leads to significantly better visual outcomes.
